### **Summary of "Long-term Monitoring of Ultratrace Nucleic Acids Using Tetrahedral Nanostructure-based NgAgo on Wearable Microneedles" (Approx. 500 Words)**  

Real-time and continuous monitoring of nucleic acid biomarkers is crucial for **personalized medicine, disease surveillance, and intensive care unit (ICU) patient management**. However, traditional methods such as **PCR and whole-genome sequencing** require **large, expensive equipment and long turnaround times**, while amplification-free methods lack **long-term stability and sensitivity**. This study presents an innovative **wearable microneedle biosensor** using **tetrahedral DNA nanostructures (TDNs) and engineered Natronobacterium gregoryi Argonaute (NgAgo)** to enable **ultrasensitive, long-term, and real-time detection of unamplified nucleic acids**.  

### **Key Features and Innovations**  
1. **Wearable Microneedle-Based Biosensor**  
   - The device integrates **a flexible wireless circuit board, a microneedle sensor, and a stretchable epidermal patch**.  
   - It allows for continuous, non-invasive nucleic acid detection in **interstitial fluid (ISF)** without the need for blood sampling.  
   
2. **Engineered NgAgo for High Sensitivity and Stability**  
   - NgAgo, guided by **single-stranded DNA (gDNA)**, binds to target nucleic acids with **high affinity**.  
   - TDNs enhance the **spatial organization** of NgAgo, improving stability and reducing steric hindrance.  
   - The system detects **cell-free DNA (cfDNA) and RNA** at concentrations as low as **0.3 fM**, outperforming many traditional biosensors.  

3. **Long-Term Monitoring and Clinical Application**  
   - The biosensor maintains **stable performance for up to 14 days** in vivo.  
   - It was validated in **sepsis-related biomarkers** (Epstein-Barr virus cfDNA, bacterial cfDNA from Staphylococcus aureus and Pseudomonas aeruginosa).  
   - In ICU-relevant models, it successfully detected cfDNA and RNA associated with infections, suggesting potential for **continuous sepsis monitoring**.  

4. **Performance and Validation**  
   - The device was tested in **animal models** and compared with **gold-standard PCR**, showing **high correlation** between real-time biosensor readings and PCR data.  
   - The wearable platform successfully monitored **dynamic nucleic acid fluctuations**, tracking disease progression and responses over time.  
   - The **integration with Bluetooth and mobile monitoring** allows for **remote, real-time health tracking**, reducing the need for invasive tests.  

### **Conclusion and Future Applications**  
This study introduces a **wearable, non-invasive, and amplification-free biosensor** capable of detecting **ultratrace nucleic acids** with unprecedented sensitivity and stability. By **combining TDNs with engineered NgAgo**, the system achieves **real-time, long-term monitoring** of cfDNA and RNA, making it highly suitable for **ICU patients, infectious disease tracking, and personalized medicine**. Future improvements will focus on **enhancing signal stability, optimizing biosensor regeneration, and integrating artificial intelligence for predictive diagnostics**.

### **Chinese Translation (摘要翻译，约500字)**  

**基于四面体纳米结构和 NgAgo 的可穿戴微针传感器用于超痕量核酸的长期监测**  

**实时和连续监测核酸生物标志物** 对 **个性化医疗、疾病监测和 ICU 患者管理** 至关重要。然而，传统的 **PCR 和全基因组测序** 需要 **昂贵的设备和长时间处理**，而无扩增方法通常缺乏 **长期稳定性和高灵敏度**。本研究开发了一种 **创新的可穿戴微针生物传感器**，结合 **四面体 DNA 纳米结构（TDNs）和工程化 NgAgo**，实现 **超高灵敏度、长期稳定、实时检测未扩增核酸**。  

### **主要特点与创新**  
1. **可穿戴微针传感器**  
   - 设备由 **柔性无线电路板、微针传感器和可拉伸皮肤贴片** 组成。  
   - 通过检测 **组织间液（ISF）中的游离 DNA/RNA**，实现连续、无创的核酸监测。  

2. **NgAgo 结合四面体 DNA 纳米结构，实现高灵敏度和稳定性**  
   - NgAgo 在 **单链 DNA（gDNA）引导下** 识别目标核酸，结合力极高。  
   - **四面体 DNA 纳米结构（TDNs）** 提供 **空间优化**，增强分子识别稳定性，减少立体障碍。  
   - 系统可检测低至 **0.3 fM** 的游离核酸，远超传统生物传感器。  

3. **长期监测和临床应用**  
   - 传感器在体内可 **稳定运行 14 天**，适用于长时间监测。  
   - 研究验证了其对 **脓毒症相关生物标志物（Epstein-Barr 病毒 cfDNA、金黄色葡萄球菌和绿脓杆菌 cfDNA）** 的检测能力。  
   - 在 **ICU 模型** 中成功检测到感染相关 cfDNA 和 RNA，表明该设备可用于 **连续脓毒症监测**。  

4. **性能测试与验证**  
   - 该设备在 **动物模型** 中测试，并与 **PCR** 进行对比，结果高度相关。  
   - 可穿戴平台成功监测了 **核酸水平的动态变化**，追踪疾病进程和生物标志物波动。  
   - **结合蓝牙和移动端监测**，实现 **远程、实时健康跟踪**，减少对侵入性检测的需求。  

### **结论与未来应用**  
本研究提出了一种 **可穿戴、无创、无需扩增的生物传感器**，可检测 **超痕量核酸**，并具有前所未有的 **灵敏度和稳定性**。通过 **TDNs 与 NgAgo 的结合**，系统实现了 **cfDNA 和 RNA 的实时、长期监测**，在 **ICU 患者管理、传染病追踪和个性化医疗** 方面具有广泛应用前景。未来的研究将着重 **优化信号稳定性、改进传感器再生能力，并结合人工智能进行预测性诊断**。
