A major quantitative trait locus affecting resistance to Tilapia lake virus in farmed Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) [Tilv 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41437-021-00461-6)

---
The study identifies a **major quantitative trait locus (QTL) on chromosome Oni22** associated with **resistance to Tilapia Lake Virus (TiLV)** in **Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus)**. A **genome-wide association study (GWAS)** using a 65K SNP array on 950 fish revealed a significant QTL, with fish homozygous for the resistance allele showing **32% lower mortality** than those with the susceptibility allele. Several candidate genes within the QTL region (**lgals17, vps52, trim29**) are linked to **immune response**. These findings provide strong potential for **marker-assisted selection (MAS)** to improve TiLV resistance, offering a **genetic solution for disease control** in tilapia aquaculture.
