### **Summary of "Pangenome-based genome inference allows efficient and accurate genotyping across a wide spectrum of variant classes" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study introduces **PanGenie**, a novel tool for **genome inference and genotyping** using a **haplotype-resolved pangenome**. Unlike traditional alignment-based methods, PanGenie utilizes **k-mer counts** and a **Hidden Markov Model (HMM)** to infer genotypes efficiently. It accurately detects **SNPs, indels, and large structural variants (SVs)**, particularly in **repetitive and complex regions**. Benchmarks on **human genomes** show **PanGenie outperforms traditional genotyping tools** in accuracy and speed. This approach enhances **population genomics and disease association studies** by enabling **faster and more accurate variant detection**.

### **Summary of "Pangenome-genotyped structural variation improves molecular phenotype mapping in cattle" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study explores the role of **pangenome-based structural variation (SV) genotyping** in enhancing **expression and splicing quantitative trait loci (e/sQTL) mapping** in cattle. Using **16 haplotype-resolved cattle assemblies**, the researchers identified **21 million small variants and 43,000 SVs** in 307 short-read samples with **PanGenie**. They validated **85% of SVs** in an independent dataset and discovered **92 SV-eQTLs and 73 SV-sQTLs**, with many linked to **transposable elements**. Including SVs improved causal variant identification, highlighting their **impact on gene expression and splicing**.

### **Summary of "Assembly of a Pangenome for Global Cattle Reveals Missing Sequences and Novel Structural Variations, Providing New Insights into Their Diversity and Evolutionary History" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **cattle pangenome** using **898 genomes from 57 breeds**, identifying **83 Mb of novel sequences** and **3.3 million structural variants (SVs)**. The **SV-based ancestry analysis** confirms the hybridization between **taurine (*Bos taurus*) and indicine (*Bos indicus*) cattle**. An insertion in the **APPL2 gene** affects **immune response and metabolism**, highlighting breed-specific selection. The study reveals **SV-rich regions linked to immunity** and **SV-poor regions in essential developmental genes**. These findings provide a **more accurate genetic reference** for **breeding, adaptation, and evolutionary studies**.

### **Summary of "A Sheep Pangenome Reveals the Spectrum of Structural Variations and Their Effects on Tail Phenotypes" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **sheep pangenome** using **15 high-quality genome assemblies from diverse breeds**, identifying **130.3 Mb of non-reference sequences and 149,158 structural variations (SVs)**. Many **SVs remain undetectable using traditional SNP-based methods**. SV与SNP之间只有59.4%的高LD The study finds **a 168-bp insertion in the 5' UTR of HOXB13**, which is significantly associated with **long-tailed sheep**, influencing gene expression and tail development. SVs are **key contributors to phenotypic variation in sheep**, highlighting their role in **domestication and trait selection**. This research provides **a comprehensive resource for understanding genetic diversity and improving sheep breeding**.

### **Summary of "A graph-based genome and pan-genome variation of the model plant Setaria" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **graph-based genome and pan-genome** of **foxtail millet (*Setaria italica*)**, a key C4 model plant, by assembling **110 genomes from diverse global accessions**. The **pan-genome contains 73,528 gene families**, with **23.8% core genes and 202,884 structural variants (SVs)**. A **366-bp presence/absence variant in the SiGW3 promoter** was linked to yield variation. Using **genetic studies across 68 traits in 13 environments**, potential candidate genes for **adaptive breeding** were identified. The findings provide insights into **millet domestication, evolution, and breeding strategies**.

### **Summary of "Cicer super-pangenome provides insights into species evolution and agronomic trait loci for crop improvement in chickpea" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **Cicer super-pangenome** by assembling **eight wild annual Cicer species** alongside cultivated chickpea genomes. It identifies **24,827 gene families**, including **14,748 core genes and 909 species-specific genes**. The research highlights **structural variations (SVs) and their roles in key agronomic traits**, such as **flowering time, vernalization, and disease resistance**. A **graph-based pangenome** was built, improving genomic diversity representation and enabling **marker-assisted breeding and gene-editing** for chickpea improvement. The findings provide **new insights into chickpea evolution, domestication, and potential for crop enhancement**.

### **Summary of "Pangenomic analysis identifies structural variation associated with heat tolerance in pearl millet" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **graph-based pan-genome** of **pearl millet** (*Pennisetum glaucum*), an essential heat-tolerant cereal, by assembling **ten chromosomal genomes** and incorporating an existing assembly. Researchers identified **424,085 structural variations (SVs)** affecting gene expression and adaptation to heat stress. The **RWP-RK transcription factor family** was found to be expanded, playing a crucial role in **heat tolerance** via regulation of **endoplasmic reticulum (ER)-related genes**. Overexpression of an **RWP-RK gene** enhanced heat tolerance, demonstrating its function in stress response. This study provides a genomic resource for **climate-resilient crop breeding**.

### **Summary of "Super-pangenome analyses highlight genomic diversity and structural variation across wild and cultivated tomato species" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **super-pangenome** of wild and cultivated tomato species, integrating **11 newly assembled chromosome-scale genomes** with previously available data. The analysis reveals **extensive structural variations (SVs)** and highlights **genetic diversity** in wild tomato species. A novel **cytochrome P450 gene deletion** is identified as a key factor affecting **fruit yield and branching architecture**. Additionally, a **graph-based genome approach** enables **SV-based GWAS**, uncovering genetic variants linked to **fruit flavor and metabolites**. These findings provide **valuable genetic resources** for **tomato breeding, domestication studies, and crop improvement**.

### **Summary of "Eight high-quality genomes reveal pan-genome architecture and ecotype differentiation of *Brassica napus*" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **high-quality pan-genome** of *Brassica napus* by sequencing and assembling **eight representative accessions**, covering **three major ecotypes**. It identifies **77.2–149.6 Mb presence/absence variations (PAVs)** and millions of small variants, with over **9.4% of genes carrying large-effect mutations**. The **PAV-based GWAS** reveals **causal structural variations** affecting **silique length, seed weight, and flowering time**, which were **missed by SNP-based GWAS**. The findings show that **structural variations, especially in *FLOWERING LOCUS C* (FLC) genes, play a critical role in ecotype differentiation**. This research provides valuable **genomic resources for breeding and crop improvement**.

### **Summary of "Genome-Wide Mapping of Structural Variations Reveals a Copy Number Variant That Determines Reproductive Morphology in Cucumber" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study constructs a **high-resolution structural variation (SV) map** of cucumber (*Cucumis sativus*), identifying **26,788 SVs** based on deep resequencing of **115 accessions**. The majority of cucumber SVs originate from **nonhomologous end-joining rearrangements** and are linked to high nucleotide diversity regions. A key finding is a **30.2-kb copy number variation (CNV)** at the **Female (F) locus**, which determines **gynoecious (female-only) flowering in cucumber**. This CNV likely arose via **microhomology-mediated break-induced replication (MMBIR)**. The SV map offers a valuable **genetic resource** for studying plant reproductive traits and breeding improvements.
