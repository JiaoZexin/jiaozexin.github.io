Applying genetic technologies to combat infectious diseases in aquaculture [2022 Ross](https://doi.org/10.1111/raq.12733)

### **Summary of "Applying Genetic Technologies to Combat Infectious Diseases in Aquaculture"**  

Infectious diseases significantly impact **aquaculture production, fish welfare, and economic sustainability**. This review explores how **genetic technologies** can enhance **disease resistance in aquaculture species**, focusing on **sea lice in salmon and white spot syndrome virus (WSSV) in shrimp**.  

### **Key Findings:**  
- **Genomic selection and selective breeding** improve disease resistance but require long-term investment.  
- **Gene editing (e.g., CRISPR-Cas9)** offers targeted genetic modifications for enhancing resistance.  
- **Transcriptomics and functional genomics** help identify key resistance genes.  
- **Epidemiological modeling** aids in predicting pathogen evolution and mitigating risks.  
- Ethical considerations and regulatory challenges must be addressed before widespread adoption.  

### **Conclusion:**  
Genetic technologies provide **novel tools for combating infectious diseases in aquaculture**, improving **sustainability and productivity**. However, their implementation requires **careful risk assessment, regulatory approval, and industry adaptation**.

### **《利用基因技术应对水产养殖中的传染病》摘要**  

水产养殖中的传染病严重影响 **生产效率、鱼类福利及经济可持续性**。本综述探讨 **基因技术** 如何提高 **水产养殖物种的抗病能力**，重点关注 **大西洋鲑海虱（sea lice）和对虾白斑综合症病毒（WSSV）**。  

### **主要发现：**  
- **基因组选择和选择性育种** 能提升抗病性，但需要长期投入。  
- **基因编辑（如 CRISPR-Cas9）** 可进行 **精准遗传改良**，提高抗病能力。  
- **转录组学和功能基因组学** 有助于鉴定关键抗病基因。  
- **流行病学建模** 预测病原体进化，并制定缓解策略。  
- **伦理和法规问题** 需在广泛应用前解决。  

### **研究结论：**  
基因技术为 **应对水产养殖传染病提供了新工具**，可提升 **产业可持续性和生产力**。然而，其推广需要 **全面的风险评估、法规批准及产业适应**。
