The study investigates the role of **structural variants (SVs)** in the genetic differentiation of **lake whitefish (Coregonus clupeaformis) species pairs (Dwarf and Normal)** using a combination of **long-read and short-read sequencing**. The researchers assembled the first **reference genomes** for both species and found that **SVs cover five times more of the genome than SNPs**. Many SVs involved **transposable element (TE) insertions**, suggesting their role in speciation. Genome-wide analyses across two lakes revealed **parallel differentiation patterns**, emphasizing the role of **SVs in species divergence**. The study highlights **the power of third-generation sequencing for speciation genomics**.

The study investigates the **structural variation (SV) landscape** and its role in **fast-growing strains** of the **Pacific oyster (Crassostrea gigas)**. Using **whole-genome alignment and resequencing of 535 individuals**, the authors identify **511,170 short SVs and 979,486 copy number variations (CNVs)**. Comparative analysis between **fast-growing strains and wild populations** reveals **514 regions under selection**, containing **746 candidate genes** linked to **growth-related traits**. Functional enrichment analysis highlights **metabolic pathways, including tryptophan and histidine metabolism**, as potential contributors to fast growth. The study provides **new insights into SVs as drivers of artificial selection** and **genetic improvement in aquaculture**.

This study explores **structural variations (SVs) in the papaya genome** and their role in **domestication, sexual reproduction, and environmental adaptation**. Researchers identified **8,083 SVs**, mainly deletions, which are non-randomly distributed across chromosomes. **1,794 genes overlapped with SVs**, with many involved in biological processes and environmental responses. **Copy number variation (CNV) analysis** revealed **genes under selection** linked to plant growth and stress resistance. Comparative analysis between **wild male and cultivated hermaphrodite papayas** highlighted SVs associated with reproductive traits, supporting the **evolution of hermaphroditism**. These findings provide **genomic resources for molecular breeding and papaya improvement**.

This study investigates **structural variations (SVs) in Guizhou indigenous pigs (GZP)** and their impact on genetic diversity and phenotypic traits. Using whole-genome resequencing of **18 GZPs**, researchers identified **39,166 SVs** covering **27.37 Mb** of the genome. Compared to European pigs (EUP), **SVs were 1.8–1.9 times more abundant in GZPs and native Chinese pigs (NPOG)**. A **hotspot on chromosome X (20 Mb region) was linked to histone modification and reproduction-related genes**. Functional analysis suggests SVs influence **gene expression, immune response, adaptability, and reproduction**, potentially explaining **low fecundity and strong adaptability** in GZPs. **51 candidate genes were identified for body configuration traits in Xiang pigs.**

A total of **28,040 SVs** were identified, distributed across all chromosomes. Several SVs were **enriched in reproduction-related pathways**, and **14 SVs were significantly associated with litter size**. These findings offer **potential genetic markers for reproductive traits**, contributing to **breeding programs for enhanced fertility in Xiang pigs**.

This study explores **genetic adaptations to hypoxia in Tibetan chickens** using **whole-genome resequencing**. Researchers identified **5,164,926 SNPs, 237,504 insertions/deletions (InDels), and 55,606 structural variations (SVs)** across the genome. Functional analysis revealed that genes such as **HIF1A, VEGF, MAPK, PPARA, and NOTCH2** are enriched in **hypoxia-related pathways**. Structural variations in coding regions suggest potential **adaptive mutations** improving oxygen transport and metabolic efficiency. The findings provide **genetic insights into high-altitude adaptation** and could aid in breeding programs to enhance hypoxia tolerance in poultry.

This study investigates **structural variations (SVs) and their impact on complex traits in *Drosophila melanogaster*** using **high-quality genome assemblies** from 14 strains. Over **20,000 euchromatic SVs** were identified, with **~40% undetectable by short-read sequencing**. SVs are **common in genes**, with 31.5% of diploid individuals carrying SVs in genes >5kb. Minor allele frequencies of SVs suggest **stronger purifying selection** than SNPs, indicating a higher likelihood of **phenotypic effects**. Additionally, SVs were **overrepresented in genes linked to quantitative trait loci (QTLs)**, supporting the idea that rare SVs contribute significantly to **complex trait variation** and missing heritability.

### **Summary of "Structural Variants in the Chinese Population and Their Impact on Phenotypes, Diseases, and Population Adaptation" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study presents a **long-read sequencing (LRS)-based structural variant (SV) analysis** of **405 unrelated Chinese individuals**, identifying **132,312 SVs**, of which **45.2% are novel**. These SVs influence **1,681 genes**, including deletions in **HBA1/HBA2/HBB**, linked to **anemia**. **Immune-related SVs** differentiate **northern and southern Chinese populations**, and **22 SVs** were significantly associated with **clinical traits**. This research provides **high-confidence SV data (3.2% false discovery rate)**, offering **insights into human adaptation and disease susceptibility**, improving **genomic reference datasets**, and advancing **precision medicine** for Chinese populations.

### **Summary of "Detection of Trait-Associated Structural Variations Using Short-Read Sequencing" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study presents **MOPline**, a computational pipeline for **accurate structural variation (SV) detection** using **short-read whole-genome sequencing (WGS)**. Applied to **3,258 BioBank Japan (BBJ) WGS datasets**, MOPline detected **~16,000 SVs per individual** and enabled **imputation in 181,622 Japanese individuals**. GWAS identified **41 trait-associated SVs**, including **8 exonic SVs and novel mobile element insertions**. This research demonstrates that **short-read WGS can effectively detect SVs**, supporting large-scale **trait and disease association studies**, especially in non-European populations.

### **Summary of "Systematic Benchmarking of Tools for Structural Variation Detection Using Short- and Long-Read Sequencing Data in Pigs" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study systematically evaluates **16 structural variation (SV) detection tools** using **short- and long-read sequencing data** from **five genetically diverse pig breeds**. **Long-read sequencing (PacBio HiFi & ONT) significantly improves SV detection**, identifying many SVs missed by short-read sequencing (Illumina WGS). **SVIM-asm (assembly-based) outperforms all tools** in accuracy and efficiency. **Alignment-based tools (cuteSV, Sniffles, SVIM, dysgu) perform well even at 5× sequencing depth**. These findings establish **high-confidence SV reference sets**, optimizing SV detection for **pig genomics, trait mapping, and selective breeding**.

### **Summary of "The Relative Power of Structural Genomic Variation versus SNPs in Explaining the Quantitative Trait Growth in the Marine Teleost *Chrysophrys auratus*" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study compares **single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) and structural variants (SVs)** in predicting **growth traits** in *Chrysophrys auratus* using **machine learning models**. Analysis of **32 fish (ages 1 and 3)** revealed that **SVs account for three times more genomic variation than SNPs**. Feature selection improved **growth prediction accuracy (up to 75%)**, with **SNPs slightly outperforming SVs**. Findings suggest that **SVs significantly influence growth** and should be included in **genomic selection for aquaculture**. **Larger datasets** and **refined machine learning models** are needed to enhance predictive accuracy.

### **Summary of "Identification and Characterisation of De Novo Germline Structural Variants in Two Commercial Pig Lines Using Trio-Based Whole Genome Sequencing" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study investigates **de novo structural variants (dnSVs) in pigs** using **whole-genome sequencing (WGS) of 37 trios**. The estimated **dnSV mutation rate is 0.108 per generation**, with **four dnSVs identified**, all located in **intronic regions**. Two mutation clusters contained multiple SV types, including **deletions, duplications, and single nucleotide variants (SNVs)**. One cluster originated from the **paternal haplotype**. Three dnSVs were **experimentally validated via PCR and sequencing**. These findings **enhance understanding of dnSV formation** and its potential impact on **livestock breeding**, while also emphasizing **limitations of short-read sequencing in SV detection**.

### **Summary of "The Population Genetics of Structural Variants in Grapevine Domestication" (Approx. 100 Words)**

This research **highlights SVs’ role in genetic diversity, adaptation, and breeding**, offering insights into grape **genomic evolution and agronomic trait improvement**.

### **Summary of "The genomic landscape of transposable elements in yeast hybrids is shaped by structural variation and genotype-specific modulation of transposition rate" (Approx. 100 Words)**

### **Summary of the "Assessment of linkage disequilibrium patterns between structural variants and single nucleotide polymorphisms in three commercial chicken populations" (Around 100 Words)**

This study examines the linkage disequilibrium (LD) between structural variants (SVs) and single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in three commercial chicken populations. Researchers generated a comprehensive SV dataset using whole-genome short-read sequencing and identified 12,294,329 SNPs, 4,301 deletions (DEL), 224 duplications (DUP), 218 inversions (INV), and 117 translocation breakpoints (BND). They found that LD between DELs and SNPs was comparable to SNP-SNP LD, while other SVs had significantly reduced LD. These findings suggest that deletions can be well represented by SNP markers, whereas separate SV calling may be necessary for DUP, INV, and BND.
