### **Summary of the "Genome-wide selection and genetic improvement during modern maize breeding" (Around 100 Words)**

This study investigates the genetic basis of modern maize breeding through a genome-wide analysis of 350 elite inbred lines from China and the U.S. Using genome-wide association studies (GWAS) and selection scans, researchers identified 160 loci associated with agronomic traits and over 1,800 genomic regions subjected to selection. Key findings include the selection for traits like reduced tassel branch number, leaf angle, and flowering time, all linked to increased planting density tolerance. The study provides insight into the parallel genetic evolution of maize in different breeding programs and demonstrates the use of a breeding-era approach for identifying key genomic selection signatures.

### **Summary of the "Chromosome genome assembly for the meagre, Argyrosomus regius, reveals species adaptations and sciaenid sex-related locus evolution" (Around 100 Words)**

This study presents the first chromosome-level genome assembly for meagre (*Argyrosomus regius*), an economically significant aquaculture species. Using a combination of long-read and short-read sequencing, researchers constructed a high-quality 696 Mb genome with 92.85% of its sequence mapped to 24 chromosomes. Phylogenetic and comparative analyses identified key adaptations in immune response, fat metabolism, and growth-related genes. Additionally, the study highlights evolutionary changes in the *dmrt1* gene region, associated with sex determination in Sciaenidae. This genomic resource enhances our understanding of meagre biology, providing valuable tools for aquaculture breeding and further evolutionary research.

### **Chinese Translation (中文翻译)**

本研究首次构建了乌鳢 (*Argyrosomus regius*) 的染色体水平基因组装，该物种在水产养殖中具有重要的经济价值。研究人员利用长读长和短读长测序技术，构建了高质量的 696 Mb 基因组，其中 92.85% 的序列被成功定位至 24 条染色体。系统发育和比较分析揭示了乌鳢在免疫反应、脂肪代谢和生长相关基因方面的关键适应性。此外，研究重点探讨了 *dmrt1* 基因区域的进化变化，该基因与石首鱼科 (Sciaenidae) 的性别决定相关。这一基因组资源将促进对乌鳢生物学的深入研究，并为水产养殖选育和进化研究提供重要工具。
