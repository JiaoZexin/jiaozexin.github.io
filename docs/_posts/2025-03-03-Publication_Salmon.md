The Atlantic salmon genome provides insights into rediploidization [Sig 2016 Salmon Ref genome](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17164)

---

This study presents a **high-quality reference genome** for **Atlantic salmon (*Salmo salar*)**, providing insights into **genome evolution after whole-genome duplication (WGD)**. Atlantic salmon underwent a **salmonid-specific fourth round of WGD (Ss4R)** ~80 million years ago. The study reveals that **large genomic reorganizations, driven by transposon-mediated expansions, played a key role in rediploidization**. Comparative analysis with pre-duplication species shows **more instances of neofunctionalization than subfunctionalization**, suggesting adaptive advantages of gene duplicates. The **genome assembly serves as a reference for other salmonids**, aiding studies on **evolution, conservation, and aquaculture improvements**.
