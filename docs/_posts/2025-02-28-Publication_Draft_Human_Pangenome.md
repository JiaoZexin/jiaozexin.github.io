### **Introduction to Pangenome**  

#### **1. What is a Pangenome?**  
A pangenome refers to the complete set of genes found within a group of related individuals or species, including both core genes (shared by all individuals) and variable genes (specific to some individuals). This concept was initially introduced in microbial genomics but has since been extended to plants, animals, and human genomics.  

#### **2. Components of a Pangenome**  
A pangenome typically consists of three parts:  
- **Core genome**: Genes shared by all individuals or strains, usually associated with fundamental biological functions and species characteristics.  
- **Accessory genome**: Genes present in some but not all individuals or strains, often linked to adaptive evolution, such as disease resistance and antibiotic resistance genes.  
- **Unique genes**: Genes found only in specific individuals or strains, often determining unique traits of those individuals.  

#### **3. Importance of Pangenome**  
- **Understanding population genetic diversity**: Traditional reference genomes often represent only a single individual and fail to capture population-level genetic variation. A pangenome provides a more comprehensive perspective.  
- **Discovering functional genes**: Variable genes are often associated with important traits such as environmental adaptation and disease resistance, which are valuable for genomic selection and breeding.  
- **Improving genome annotation**: Pangenomes help to fill in missing genes and structural variations, enhancing genome annotation accuracy.  
- **Facilitating evolutionary and ecological studies**: Comparing pangenomes across different individuals or species can provide insights into evolutionary patterns and ecological adaptation.  

#### **4. Methods for Constructing a Pangenome**  
The construction of a pangenome typically follows these approaches:  
- **De novo assembly + alignment**: Genomes of multiple individuals are assembled separately and then aligned to identify shared and unique genes.  
- **Reference-based approach**: Genomic sequences from multiple individuals are mapped to a reference genome to detect structural variations and novel genes.  
- **Graph-based pangenome**: A graph structure is used to represent multiple genomes, effectively integrating structural variation information. This approach has been widely applied in recent human and crop genomics research.  

#### **5. Applications of Pangenome in Aquaculture**  
In aquaculture, pangenome studies can contribute to:  
- Understanding the genetic diversity of farmed fish populations and providing a comprehensive genetic background for breeding.  
- Identifying variable genes associated with disease resistance, adaptability, and growth traits, thereby improving molecular breeding efficiency.  
- Enhancing the accuracy of structural variation (SV) detection and supporting the development of high-quality genomic reference resources.  

#### **6. Relationship Between Pangenome and Structural Variation (SV)**  
Pangenome studies can reveal population-level structural variations, such as gene deletions, insertions, duplications, and inversions. These structural variations are often linked to key traits like disease resistance and growth rate. Therefore, pangenome analysis provides a broader data foundation for studying SVs, improving the precision of SV detection.

### **泛基因组（Pangenome）简介**  

#### **1. 什么是泛基因组？**  
泛基因组（Pangenome）指的是一组相关个体或物种的所有基因集合，包括核心基因（所有个体共有）和可变基因（部分个体特有）。这一概念最初用于微生物基因组研究，后来扩展到动植物和人类基因组研究中。

#### **2. 泛基因组的组成**  
泛基因组通常由以下三个部分组成：  
- **核心基因组（Core genome）**：所有个体或菌株共有的基因，通常与基本生物学功能和物种特征相关。  
- **可变基因组（Accessory genome）**：部分个体或菌株特有的基因，包括适应性进化相关基因，如抗病基因、抗生素抗性基因等。  
- **独特基因（Unique genes）**：只存在于某个特定个体或菌株中的基因，通常决定个体特有的性状。  

#### **3. 泛基因组的意义**  
- **解析种群遗传多样性**：传统的“参考基因组”往往只代表单个个体，无法充分捕捉种群内的遗传变异，而泛基因组提供了更全面的视角。  
- **挖掘功能基因**：可变基因往往与环境适应、疾病抗性等重要性状相关，有助于基因组选择和育种。  
- **改进基因组注释**：泛基因组能够更好地补充基因缺失和结构变异，提高基因注释的完整性。  
- **促进进化和生态研究**：通过比较不同个体或物种的泛基因组，可以研究进化模式和生态适应性。  

#### **4. 泛基因组的构建方法**  
泛基因组构建通常采用以下策略：  
- **基因组组装后比对（De novo assembly + alignment）**：对多个个体进行基因组组装，再进行比对和注释，构建基因集。  
- **参考基因组+变异检测（Reference-based approach）**：基于参考基因组比对多个个体的序列，识别结构变异和新基因。  
- **图结构泛基因组（Graph-based pangenome）**：采用图结构表示多个基因组，能够更有效地整合结构变异信息，近年来广泛应用于人类和农作物基因组研究。  

#### **5. 泛基因组在水产养殖中的应用**  
在水产养殖中，泛基因组研究有助于：  
- 解析养殖鱼类的种群遗传多样性，为育种提供更全面的遗传背景信息。  
- 发现与抗病性、适应性、生长性状相关的可变基因，提高分子育种效率。  
- 提升结构变异检测的准确性，为构建高质量的基因组参考资源提供支撑。  

#### **6. 泛基因组与结构变异（SV）的关系**  
泛基因组研究能够揭示种群内的结构变异，如基因缺失、插入、重复、倒位等。这些结构变异往往与重要的性状有关，例如抗病性或生长速率。因此，泛基因组可以为结构变异的研究提供更全面的数据支持，提高SV分析的精度。

### **Summary of "A Draft Human Pangenome Reference" (Nature, 2023)**  

#### **Introduction**  
The **Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (HPRC)** presents the first draft of a **human pangenome reference**, built from **47 phased, diploid genome assemblies** of genetically diverse individuals. These assemblies provide a **highly accurate representation (99%)** of the expected genome sequence and improve upon the existing **GRCh38 reference genome** by incorporating **119 million base pairs** of euchromatic polymorphic sequences and **1,115 gene duplications**. The pangenome approach enhances variant detection, reducing small variant discovery errors by **34%** and increasing structural variant detection by **104%**.  

#### **Background & Need for a Pangenome**  
The **human reference genome (GRCh38)** has been fundamental to genomics for over 20 years. However, it represents a mosaic of haplotypes, with gaps and computationally simulated sequences making up **6.7% of the genome**. The **Telomere-to-Telomere (T2T) Consortium** recently completed a **haploid human genome (T2T-CHM13)**, adding missing sequences and resolving some limitations of GRCh38. However, **no single genome can represent human genetic diversity**, as previous studies indicate that large structural variants (SVs) are missing from reference-based analyses. The absence of these alternative alleles has led to **the under-detection of two-thirds of structural variants** in studies relying on short-read sequencing.  

To **overcome reference bias**, the **pangenome** approach is introduced, aiming to **incorporate diverse genomic sequences into a single reference framework**.  

#### **Methods: Building the Human Pangenome**  
The study assembled **47 diverse diploid genomes** using **long-read sequencing (PacBio HiFi, Oxford Nanopore), optical mapping, and Hi-C sequencing**. The samples were selected based on **global genetic diversity**, ensuring a broad representation of human populations. The genomes were assembled with **Trio-Hifiasm**, a tool that leverages long reads and parental data to generate near-complete, **phased diploid genome assemblies**.  

A **quality control pipeline** ensured the completeness, contiguity, base-level accuracy, and phasing precision of the assemblies. The **N50 contig length** (a measure of assembly quality) was comparable to GRCh38. The average base quality (QV score) was **53.57**, corresponding to **one error per 227,509 bases**.  

### **Key Findings**  
#### **1. Improved Genome Completeness & Accuracy**  
- The human pangenome added **119 million base pairs** beyond GRCh38, with **90 million base pairs coming from structural variations**.  
- Only **0.88% of each assembly was deemed unreliable**, showing that the new reference is **highly robust**.  
- The pangenome captures **structural variants (SVs) more effectively**, significantly improving the representation of **copy number variations (CNVs), segmental duplications, and variable number tandem repeats (VNTRs)**.  

#### **2. Annotation of the Pangenome**  
- **99.07% of protein-coding genes** and **98.16% of non-coding genes** were annotated in the pangenome, ensuring comprehensive functional coverage.  
- **1,115 gene duplications** were identified, with some genes showing **high copy number polymorphism** across populations.  

#### **3. Graph-Based Pangenome Representation**  
- The pangenome was represented using a **graph-based model**, allowing multiple genome sequences to be incorporated without a linear reference bias.  
- Three methods were used: **Minigraph, Minigraph-Cactus (MC), and PanGenome Graph Builder (PGGB)**, each capturing different levels of genomic variation.  

#### **4. Improved Structural Variant Detection**  
- Compared to GRCh38-based workflows, the **human pangenome reference increased the detection of structural variants by 104%**.  
- The pangenome captured **over 67,000 SVs**, many of which had been **missed by traditional linear reference-based methods**.  

#### **5. Applications & Future Impact**  
- **Improved Short-Read Variant Discovery**: Using the pangenome as a reference **reduced small variant discovery errors by 34%**, significantly enhancing variant calling accuracy.  
- **Better Representation of Complex Genomic Loci**: The study analyzed **highly complex structural variations** at important genes like **RHD-RHCE (blood group), CYP2D6 (drug metabolism), and HLA-A (immune response)**, revealing **new haplotypes** and previously unknown structural variations.  
- **More Accurate Population Genetics Studies**: By incorporating diverse genomes, the pangenome allows for **better representation of underrepresented populations**, reducing reference bias in genetic association studies.  

### **Conclusion**  
The **draft human pangenome reference** is a major advancement in genomics, **overcoming the limitations of a single linear reference genome**. By integrating **diverse human genomes**, it enhances the accuracy of **variant detection, structural variation analysis, and genome-wide association studies (GWAS)**. As the **HPRC expands to include 350 individuals and 700 haplotypes**, the pangenome will provide an **even more complete and unbiased view of human genetic diversity**, paving the way for **personalized medicine, better disease risk prediction, and improved genomic research methodologies**.

### **关键知识点及核心概念（基于《A Draft Human Pangenome Reference》）**  

#### **1. 泛基因组（Pangenome）**  
- **定义**：泛基因组指的是一组相关个体或物种的所有基因集合，包括**核心基因（core genome）**（所有个体共有）和**可变基因（accessory genome）**（部分个体特有）。  
- **人类泛基因组的意义**：相比于单一参考基因组（如GRCh38），泛基因组能够更全面地表达人类基因组的多样性，减少参考基因组偏倚（Reference bias）。  

#### **2. 参考基因组的局限性及改进**  
- **GRCh38（人类参考基因组）**  
  - 传统的线性参考基因组，如**GRCh38**，尽管被广泛使用，但**无法完整表达人类基因组的多样性**，尤其是在**结构变异（SVs）**方面。  
  - 现有GRCh38中仍有 **210 Mb 的缺失或未知序列（占6.7%）**，影响研究的全面性。  
- **T2T-CHM13（端粒到端粒参考基因组）**  
  - **Telomere-to-Telomere（T2T）**项目完成了第一个完整的单倍体人类基因组（T2T-CHM13），填补了部分参考基因组的空白。  
  - 但单倍体基因组仍不足以代表人类群体的**遗传多样性**，仍需泛基因组来更全面地展现变异。  

#### **3. 结构变异（Structural Variations, SVs）**  
- **定义**：SVs指基因组中**大于50bp**的结构性变化，包括**插入（insertions）、缺失（deletions）、倒位（inversions）、复制（duplications）**等。  
- **重要性**：SVs对基因表达和表型的影响远大于**单核苷酸多态性（SNPs）**，但由于传统线性参考基因组的限制，**超过2/3的SVs未被发现**。  
- **人类泛基因组检测到的SVs**：本研究发现**约90 Mb的新序列**来自SVs，并将SV检测能力提升了**104%**。  

#### **4. 图结构泛基因组（Graph-based Pangenome）**  
- **概念**：基因组数据被表示为**图结构（graph）**，而不是单一线性序列，使得多个个体的序列可以在同一结构中表达。  
- **优势**：  
  - 能够**同时存储多个个体的基因组变异**，避免线性参考基因组的偏倚。  
  - 能够准确捕捉**复杂的SV**，如**重复序列、拷贝数变异（CNVs）、串联重复（VNTRs）**等。  
- **本研究采用的图结构构建方法**：  
  - **Minigraph**：基于GRCh38，从单一参考序列出发，逐步添加SV信息。  
  - **Minigraph-Cactus（MC）**：结合Minigraph方法，并使用Cactus算法进行基因组比对，提高小变异（small variants）检测能力。  
  - **PanGenome Graph Builder（PGGB）**：基于全基因组比对构建，能更好地保留基因组的结构信息。  

#### **5. 变异检测及改进（Variant Calling）**  
- **泛基因组对短读测序（Short-Read Sequencing）的改进**  
  - 传统的**基于GRCh38**的方法在变异检测方面存在**高错误率**，尤其在**SV检测**方面。  
  - 使用泛基因组比对后，**小变异（small variants）检测错误减少34%**，SV检测提高**104%**。  
- **不同的变异检测工具及其应用**：  
  - **DeepVariant**：基于深度学习的变异检测工具，广泛用于短读测序数据的SNP/Indel检测。  
  - **DeepTrio**：基于DeepVariant，用于三代家系（parent-child trio）变异分析。  
  - **Giraffe**：基于图泛基因组的比对工具，相比BWA-MEM能更精确地识别变异。  

#### **6. 基因复制数变异（Copy Number Variations, CNVs）**  
- **CNVs**指某些基因在不同个体中存在**不同拷贝数**的情况，对基因表达和性状的影响较大。  
- **本研究检测到1,115个基因的拷贝数变异**，并发现某些**高度多态性基因（如GPRIN2和SPDYE2）**存在1~4个额外拷贝。  

#### **7. 泛基因组在医学和群体遗传学中的应用**  
- **改善医学基因组学研究**  
  - 传统的线性参考基因组无法捕捉某些**重要医学相关基因（如CYP2D6、HLA-A等）的复杂结构变异**，泛基因组能更精确地识别这些变异。  
  - 泛基因组能更好地**发现与疾病相关的新等位基因**，有助于**精准医学**。  
- **提升群体遗传学研究**  
  - 泛基因组减少了研究中**基于单一参考基因组的偏倚**，能更公平地代表**不同种群的遗传变异**，有助于全球范围内的遗传研究。  

### **总结**  
这篇文章的核心贡献在于：  
1. **提出并构建了人类泛基因组参考（HPRC）**，比GRCh38更完整，更能代表人类基因组多样性。  
2. **引入图结构泛基因组**，提高结构变异检测能力，使SV检测增加**104%**，小变异错误减少**34%**。  
3. **应用泛基因组改进变异检测**，特别是在短读测序数据分析中，比对准确度明显提升。  
4. **发现大量基因复制数变异（CNVs）**，揭示了个体间基因组结构的巨大差异。  
5. **对医学和群体遗传学研究具有深远影响**，有助于**精准医学、疾病研究和遗传变异分析**。

### **Summary of "The Human Pangenome Project: A Global Resource to Map Genomic Diversity" (Nature, 2022)**  

#### **Introduction**  
The **Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (HPRC)** aims to create a comprehensive **human pangenome reference**, incorporating diverse global genomic sequences to **overcome the limitations of the current reference genome (GRCh38)**. The existing reference is a **linear composite**, mainly derived from a single individual, leading to **reference bias** and **incomplete representation of human genomic diversity**. The HPRC leverages **graph-based genomic representation** to construct a more **accurate, diverse, and comprehensive reference genome**, facilitating **better disease research, improved variant detection, and advancements in precision medicine**.  

### **Why a Pangenome Reference is Needed**  
- The **GRCh38 reference genome** contains errors, rare structural configurations, and large gaps in highly **repetitive and polymorphic regions**, limiting its utility for **structural variation detection**.  
- Traditional methods fail to capture **more than 70% of structural variations (SVs)**, leading to inaccuracies in gene-disease association studies and variant calling.  
- The HPRC introduces **graph-based genome representation**, allowing for **multiple haplotypes** and more accurate alignments.  
- New sequencing technologies (e.g., **long-read sequencing and HiFi sequencing**) now enable **high-quality, phased diploid genome assemblies**, making the construction of a **pangenome reference practical**.  

### **Goals and Strategies of the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (HPRC)**  
The HPRC’s primary objectives include:  
1. **Building a pangenome reference** with **at least 350 reference-quality diploid human genomes (700 haplotypes total)**.  
2. **Ensuring global genetic diversity representation** by including individuals from diverse **biogeographical backgrounds**.  
3. **Developing a graph-based genomic reference** that can better represent **structural variants, segmental duplications, and alternative haplotypes**.  
4. **Enhancing variant detection** to improve the identification of **disease-related genetic variations**.  
5. **Providing an ethical framework** to ensure the fair and inclusive representation of populations in genomic research.  

### **Methodology: Constructing the Human Pangenome**  
- **Sample Selection**: Initial individuals were chosen from the **1000 Genomes Project (1KGP)** and other global initiatives, ensuring **broad genetic diversity**.  
- **Sequencing Technologies**: The HPRC relies on **PacBio HiFi, Oxford Nanopore, Hi-C, and optical mapping** to assemble **high-quality, phased diploid genomes**.  
- **Graph-Based Representation**: Instead of a single linear reference, **pangenome graphs** incorporate multiple genome sequences, reducing **mapping errors and reference bias**.  
- **Bioinformatics & Data Integration**: The project integrates data from **multi-platform sequencing, phasing techniques, and machine-learning-based variant detection** to **enhance accuracy**.  

### **Key Findings**  
#### **1. Improved Structural Variant Detection**  
- The **pangenome reference significantly increases structural variant detection (by 104%)**, capturing **missing insertions, deletions, duplications, and inversions**.  
- It helps to **resolve complex regions**, including **segmental duplications, tandem repeats, and CNVs (copy number variations)**, which were poorly represented in GRCh38.  

#### **2. Better Representation of Global Genomic Diversity**  
- Unlike GRCh38, which is biased toward European ancestry, the **pangenome incorporates genetic diversity across global populations**, reducing **ancestry-related bias in genomic studies**.  
- The HPRC collaborates with initiatives like **H3Africa, the Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH), and Indigenous genomic programs** to **ensure ethical data inclusion**.  

#### **3. Enhanced Accuracy in Genomic Analyses**  
- **Small variant calling errors are reduced by 34%** compared to GRCh38.  
- The **pangenome improves genome-wide association studies (GWAS)** by reducing false positives and false negatives in **genotype-phenotype correlations**.  
- Provides a **better foundation for disease-related gene studies**, particularly in **cancer genetics, pharmacogenomics, and rare disease research**.  

### **Applications of the Pangenome Reference**  
#### **1. Precision Medicine & Clinical Genetics**  
- Helps identify **rare and complex disease-causing variants** missed in **traditional reference-based methods**.  
- Improves diagnosis and treatment strategies for **cancers, neurodevelopmental disorders, and inherited diseases** by providing more **complete genetic information**.  
- Enables **more accurate pharmacogenomic predictions**, guiding **drug response assessments based on individual genetic variations**.  

#### **2. Population Genetics & Evolutionary Studies**  
- Allows for **more accurate reconstructions of human evolutionary history**, providing insights into **genetic adaptations and ancestry**.  
- Facilitates the study of **population-specific genetic traits**, enhancing **comparative genomics research**.  

#### **3. Genomic Data Sharing & Open Science**  
- The HPRC ensures **open access to sequencing data and genome assemblies** via platforms like **AnVIL, UCSC Genome Browser, Ensembl, and NCBI**.  
- It promotes **data-sharing policies aligned with the FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) and CARE (Collective benefit, Authority, Responsibility, Ethics) principles** for ethical genomics research.  

### **Challenges and Future Directions**  
#### **Challenges**  
- **Technical Barriers**: The **integration of graph-based representations** into mainstream genomic analysis tools remains complex.  
- **Ethical Considerations**: Ensuring **proper representation of diverse populations** while **respecting ethical guidelines** is crucial.  
- **Adoption & Implementation**: Transitioning from GRCh38 to a pangenome model will require **broad adoption by researchers, clinicians, and industry stakeholders**.  

#### **Future Directions**  
- Expand the reference set to **350 individuals (700 haplotypes)**, increasing **global genomic diversity representation**.  
- Improve **sequencing and phasing methods**, focusing on **high-resolution variant detection** and better representation of **centromeric and telomeric regions**.  
- Develop **user-friendly tools** for **graph-based genome analysis** to facilitate broader adoption in **biomedical research and clinical genetics**.  
- Strengthen **collaborations with global health and research institutions** to ensure **fair and inclusive representation of human genomic diversity**.  

### **Conclusion**  
The **Human Pangenome Project** represents a **major step forward in genomics**, providing a **comprehensive, diverse, and accurate human reference genome**. By moving beyond a single linear reference, the **pangenome reference enhances variant detection, improves disease association studies, and promotes equity in genomic research**. Its impact spans **precision medicine, evolutionary biology, and global health**, setting the foundation for the next generation of **genomic discoveries and biomedical innovations**.
