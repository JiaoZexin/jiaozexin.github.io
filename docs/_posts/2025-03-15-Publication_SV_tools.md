### **Summary of "Detection of Mosaic and Population-Level Structural Variants with Sniffles2" (Approx. 100 Words)**  

This study introduces **Sniffles2**, an advanced tool for detecting **structural variations (SVs)** in **long-read sequencing data**. Sniffles2 improves upon previous SV callers by implementing **repeat-aware clustering, fast consensus sequence generation, and adaptive filtering**, making it **11.8× faster and 29% more accurate**. It enables **population-scale SV calling** with **fully genotyped VCFs** and detects **mosaic SVs**, particularly in neurological disease studies. The tool successfully identified **complex SVs around MECP2** and mosaic variants in **multiple system atrophy (MSA) brain tissue**. Sniffles2 expands the potential for **long-read SV detection in genetic diseases and population genomics**.
